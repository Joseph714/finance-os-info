Terms of use

Updated October 21, 2026

Finance OS is an evolving personal desktop tool for organizing financial evidence. It is operated by its owner and is not offered here as a public financial account service.

Purpose and access

Use Finance OS only with accounts, messages and documents you are authorized to access. Google account access requires the account holder's consent. This website does not provide access to the desktop application or its financial records.

Review and accuracy

AI-generated categories, extracted transactions and explanations can be incomplete or incorrect. Review material decisions against original documents and official bank records. Purchase notifications are provisional evidence; they do not establish a bank balance or prove that every transaction has been captured.

Finance OS is not a bank or a provider of investment, tax or legal advice. It does not connect directly to banks, initiate payments or execute transfers. Its records do not change official bank records.

Availability and backups

The application depends on the owner's computer, local software, connectivity and authorized Gmail access. It does not process new work while the computer is off. Authorization may expire or be revoked, and external services may change their availability or requirements.

The owner is responsible for maintaining suitable backups, safeguarding recovery phrases and testing restoration. The tool remains experimental until the owner accepts its performance for their intended use.

Privacy and account control

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. The owner can stop the local services and revoke Google access through Google Account permissions. Previously downloaded records and separately stored backups require separate management.

Contact

For application information, contact josgonzalez330@gmail.com. Do not include credentials or financial documents in a support message.
