Finance OS helps its owner organize purchase notifications, review spending categories and reconcile monthly statements using a local application and local AI.

The workflow

From purchase alerts
to reviewed records.

Purchase alerts provide a provisional view during the month. Statements provide evidence for later reconciliation. Suggestions remain subject to validation, review and the owner's configured rules.

  1. 01

    Receive

    Read financial notifications and statement attachments from an authorized Gmail mailbox.

  2. 02

    Review

    Prepare local category proposals and ask the owner about ambiguous purchases or required splits.

  3. 03

    Reconcile

    Compare notifications with imported statements, preserve source evidence and avoid counting the same purchase twice.

Google account access

Why Finance OS requests Gmail access

The desktop application requests read-only Gmail access to retrieve messages and attachments for this finance workflow. A dedicated mailbox containing forwarded financial messages is the intended setup.

The permission applies to the whole authorized mailbox. Message filters select what the collector processes; they do not narrow the OAuth permission. Finance OS does not request permission to send, edit or delete Gmail messages.

Read the Google data disclosure →

The finance workspace stays local.

This website contains public application information only. It has no sign-in, uploads or connection to the owner's financial workspace. Gmail keeps the mailbox copies hosted by Google; downloaded financial evidence and AI processing run on the owner's computer.

Finance OS does not connect directly to banks, initiate payments or execute transfers.
